ZiYa is a Chinese character app for children. It teaches one character at a time: a large Kaiti character with pinyin, the character, a word and a sentence read aloud, then a stroke animation. Learned characters move into review and come back after a while.

![ZiYa learning page on Mac](assets/shot-learn.webp)

## Features

- **Library**: 3,500 common characters in 46 units, from a starter stage through Grade 6, or grouped into 100+ themes such as body, animals, plants, food and nature. Search by character, pinyin or word.
- **Character cards**: large Kaiti character with pinyin; the character, a word and a sentence are read aloud, with replay and slow playback; a stroke animation follows. Some common characters have natural-sounding voice clips (AI-generated); the rest use the system's offline voice.
- **Review**: shuffle review, or review by theme or grade; learned characters come back at intervals.
- **Listen & pick quiz**: a parent sets the number of questions (5–50) and the level (Basic / Intermediate / Challenge). Questions only use characters the child has learned. Mistakes are recorded quietly and reviewed at the end.
- **Growth report and parent center**: progress and past mistakes; daily goal, quiz defaults and review style.
- **Five skins**: Sprout, Ink, Island, Candy, Starlight.
- **Keyboard on Mac**: Space to play / pause, ← → to switch characters, R to replay, S to play strokes, ⌘F to find.

The app's interface and content are in Simplified Chinese.

## Privacy

No account and no internet connection needed. Progress, settings and quiz history stay on the device. No ads, analytics, third-party tracking or in-app purchases.
